RESULT: NOT DETECTED

No LLDP/CDP advertisement observed

This result means LANVEXA did not observe a neighbor advertisement during the listening period. It is not automatically a failed network test.

WHAT REMAINS USEFUL

Link, addressing, gateway, DNS, and configured TCP results remain valid observations even when no neighbor information appears.

Possible conditions

  • LLDP and CDP are disabled on the neighboring device.
  • Advertisements are not enabled on the endpoint-facing interface.
  • Frames are filtered or not forwarded toward the endpoint.
  • The advertisement interval is longer than the observation window.
  • The wired adapter, driver, dock, or capture configuration does not expose the frame.
  • The connected device does not support or send LLDP/CDP.

These are possibilities, not diagnoses. LANVEXA cannot determine the actual reason solely from the absence of an advertisement.

Reasonable next steps

  1. Confirm the intended wired interface is selected.
  2. Allow for the device’s advertisement interval.
  3. Confirm ordinary connectivity results independently of discovery.
  4. If authorized, ask the network team whether LLDP/CDP is enabled toward that endpoint.
  5. Use another approved capture or switch-side method when further verification is required.

Do not conclude

Do not treat “not detected” as proof of a dead switch, wrong VLAN, broken cable, or failed port.
