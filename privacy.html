Privacy notice

A short statement about the website’s beta-access form. It does not describe an unreleased application’s behavior.

KNOWN

The beta form asks for one required field: an email address. A hidden anti-spam field is also present.

Why the email is requested

The address is intended for communication about beta availability, installation information, and feedback expectations. The form does not enroll the visitor in a general marketing list.

Form processing

The static-site implementation is configured for Netlify Forms. Once deployed, submission processing, spam detection, and hosting logs depend on the final Netlify account configuration.
